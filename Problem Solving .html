Business Context

The Golden Fork is a fictional restaurant exploring how digital technology can improve its business operations. Traditional telephone bookings and paper-based feedback can be time-consuming and difficult to organise.

The goal of this digital transformation project is to provide customers with a convenient online contact and booking request form, strengthen social media engagement, and improve communication. The proposed website demonstrates a simple, cost-effective solution without requiring a live database.

Privacy, Security & Data Protection

Protecting customer information is an important part of digital transformation. This demonstration applies several privacy-conscious principles.

  • Data minimisation: Only request information needed to handle the enquiry.
  • Transparency: Explain how form information will be used before customers submit it.
  • Data protection: A real production website should use HTTPS, appropriate access controls, and secure handling of customer information.
  • GDPR: The restaurant should establish a lawful basis for processing, provide an appropriate privacy notice, and follow applicable data protection and retention requirements.
  • Cookie awareness: This demonstration does not use tracking cookies or analytics. Non-essential tracking, if added later, must be handled in accordance with applicable consent rules.

This page is an educational prototype, not a complete legal compliance solution. A real restaurant must publish its own privacy notice and assess its actual data processing.

This demonstration does not use tracking cookies. If optional analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, the website will need an appropriate consent mechanism.
