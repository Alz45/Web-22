About My Projects

This page showcases projects that demonstrate my ability to identify problems, develop practical solutions and evaluate the results of my work. The projects include individual and group-based activities and demonstrate a range of technical, analytical and professional skills.

Each project follows a similar structure: identifying the problem, explaining my role, describing the solution, evaluating the outcome and identifying the skills and approaches used.

Project 1: Business Intelligence in Practice

Project Overview

This project involved working in a group to create a presentation that analysed Business Intelligence (BI) data and proposed an innovative, data-driven solution to a real business challenge.

My Role

My role was to create and design the presentation slides, explaining the business problem, the proposed solution, and its benefits and drawbacks.

Problem Addressed

One of the main challenges was delivering the full presentation because not all group members were present at the time.

Solution

The final solution was to share the scripts among the group members who were present, allowing us to deliver the presentation as effectively as possible.

Outcome

The project resulted in an outstanding group presentation that analysed business data and proposed an innovative, data-driven solution to a real business challenge.

Project 2: Business Intelligence and Dashboards

Project Overview

This project involved working with an international stakeholder to design and develop a decision-making process. Its purpose was to support an international agency in making effective decisions.

My Role

I worked with the information provided by the international agency to plan and develop the dashboard, helping organise the dashboard results so stakeholders can find the information easily.

Problem Addressed

The stakeholder needed a dashboard that compared results across different income levels and clearly explained why the comparison was fair.

Solution

I created a dashboard with clear information for the stakeholder, using different chart types to represent key points. I also created a guidance page to help the stakeholder navigate and interpret the dashboard effectively.

Outcome

The dashboard provides a clear overview of the information, making it easier for the stakeholder to find and use key details.
